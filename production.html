Part Production

We do production of plastic parts from the molds we make or from your existing molds. If we are making the mold, we quote the mold and the production together.

Request a Quote WhatsApp Us

Production Options

From Our Mold

After the samples are approved, production starts on the same mold. One quote covers the mold and the parts.

From Your Mold

You can send us your existing mold. We check it, run trials and start production after you approve the samples.

Our Production Service

  • Material as per your grade, or supplied by you
  • Sample approval before production
  • Quality checks during production
  • Export packing as per your requirement
  • Shipping and documentation on request

Single Point of Contact

Design, mold and production are handled by the same team, so you have one point of contact for the full project.

You can also start with only the design.

Production Process

  1. Quote

    Mold and parts quoted together, or parts only if you send your mold.

  2. Sample Approval

    You approve the samples before production starts.

  3. Production

    Parts are produced on our injection molding machine.

  4. Inspection and Packing

    Parts are checked against the approved sample and packed.

  5. Dispatch

    Parts are dispatched to your address with the documents.

Need Parts Made?

Send us the part details, material and quantity, and we will send you a quote within 24 hours.
