Two ways to start

From a mold we build

The mold we design and build goes straight into production once you approve the samples. One quote covers the mold and the parts.

From your own mold

Send us your existing mold. We check it, run trial shots and start production when you approve the parts.

What is included

  • Material bought to the grade you specify, or supplied by you
  • Samples for approval before the production run starts
  • Checks during production against your approved sample
  • Packing to your instructions, made for export
  • Shipping documents and freight arranged on request

One partner for the whole job

Design, mold and parts from the same team means one person to call when something needs to change. The engineer who designed your part is the one checking your samples.

Prefer to start smaller? Every project can begin with the design only.

How production works

  1. Quote

    Parts and mold quoted together, or parts only if you send your mold.

  2. Samples approved

    You approve the first parts before the run starts.

  3. Production

    Parts are molded on our injection molding machine.

  4. Checks and packing

    Parts are checked against the sample and packed for export.

  5. Shipping

    Sent to your address with the shipping documents.
