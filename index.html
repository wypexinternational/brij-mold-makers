About Us

Brij Mold Makers is a GST-registered company working on plastic part design, injection molds and molded part production.

Our design work is led by Yuvraj Sehgal, a mechanical engineer with a tool and die background. He has worked on plastic parts and molds for automotive, household appliance, FMCG and electronic-controller products.

We design in SolidWorks, Siemens NX and AutoCAD, and run mold flow analysis in Autodesk Moldflow.

FAQs

What can you start from?

A 3D file, a 2D drawing, a physical sample or photos with dimensions.

What is in the free DFM check?

We check your part for wall thickness, draft, undercuts, sink marks and gate location, and send suggested changes with the quote.

Can you produce parts from our existing mold?

Yes. We run production from molds we build and from molds supplied by the customer.

Which file formats do you deliver?

Native SolidWorks or NX files, STEP, IGES and PDF drawings. Other formats on request.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes, before you share any files. We never show client work.

Who owns the design and the mold?

You do, once they are paid for.
