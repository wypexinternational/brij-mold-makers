Checked Before It Is Made

Every design is checked so it works the first time it is made.

  • Manufacturability

    Can be machined, molded or fabricated without changes.

  • Strength

    Takes the load it will see in use.

  • Fitment

    Fits the parts it assembles with.

  • Tolerances

    Realistic tolerances, so parts are not costly to make.

3D Printing

See and test your design before production. We 3D print parts to check size, shape and fit. Resin prints and machined prototypes are also available.

About Us

Brij Mold Makers is a GST-registered engineering design and manufacturing company.

Our design work is led by Yuvraj Sehgal, a mechanical engineer with a tool and die background and 6–7 years of design experience. We have worked on products for the automotive, household appliance, FMCG and electronics industries.

We design in SolidWorks, Siemens NX and AutoCAD.

FAQs

What can you start from?

A sketch, photos, a physical sample, a 2D drawing or an existing 3D model.

Which file formats do you deliver?

Native SolidWorks or NX files, STEP, IGES, STL and PDF drawings. Other formats on request.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes, before you share any files. We never show client work.

Who owns the design?

You do, once it is paid for.
