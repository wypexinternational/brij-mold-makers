Design Checks We Do

Before a mold design is released for manufacturing, we check it for the common problems that come up during molding. This saves time and cost at the trial stage.

  • DFM (Design for Manufacturing)

    We check draft angles, wall thickness, undercuts and the parting line so the part can be molded without problems.

  • Mold Flow Analysis

    Filling, injection pressure, weld lines and air traps are checked using Autodesk Moldflow.

  • Gate Location

    The gate position is selected for proper filling and to avoid gate marks on visible surfaces.

  • Sink Marks

    Thick sections are found and corrected in the design to avoid sink marks on the part.

  • Air Venting

    Air trap areas are identified and vents are added to avoid burn marks and short shots.

  • Cooling and Warpage

    Cooling lines are designed to control warpage and reduce the cycle time.

  • Shrinkage and Tolerances

    Material shrinkage is applied in the mold so the parts come out to drawing size.

  • Strength

    Wall thickness, ribs and bosses are checked so the part can take its working load.

  • Fitment

    Fitment with mating parts is checked in the assembly and on a 3D printed sample.

FAQs

What does mold design cost?

Mold design starts at $200. The final price depends on the part size, the number of cavities and mold features like sliders and lifters. Send us your part and we will quote within 24 hours.

Can I order only the design?

Yes. You will get the native files and STEP files, and you can get the mold made by any toolroom.

Which file formats do you accept?

STEP, IGES, Parasolid, SolidWorks, STL, DWG, DXF and PDF. If you do not have a CAD file, we can work from a sample or photos.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes. We sign an NDA before receiving your files. We never show client work on our website or social media.

How will we communicate during the project?

Through WhatsApp, email or video call, as per your convenience.
