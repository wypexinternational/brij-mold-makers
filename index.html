Every design is checked for these problems

Most mold problems can be seen on screen. Fixing one there takes hours. Fixing it in steel takes weeks and a second trial.

  • Design for manufacturing

    Draft angles, wall thickness, undercuts and the parting line, checked so the part can be molded and released cleanly.

  • Mold flow analysis

    How the plastic fills the mold, where weld lines form and how much pressure it needs. Run in Autodesk Moldflow.

  • Gate location

    Where the plastic enters the part, placed for an even fill and to keep marks off the faces people see.

  • Sink marks

    Thick areas shrink as they cool and leave dents on the surface. We find them and change the design.

  • Air vents

    Where air gets trapped as the mold fills, causing burn marks or parts that do not fill, and where to vent it.

  • Cooling and warpage

    Cooling channels placed so the part comes out flat, in size, and in the shortest cycle time.

  • Shrinkage and tolerances

    Material shrinkage built into the mold, so the finished part matches your drawing.

  • Strength

    Walls, ribs and bosses checked so the part can take the load it will see in use.

  • Fit

    Each part checked against the parts it assembles with, first in CAD and then on a printed sample.

Who we are

Brij Mold Makers is a design and mold company in Focal Point, Jalandhar, the industrial area of Punjab, India. We are GST registered and work with clients in India and abroad.

Design is led by Yuvraj Sehgal, a mechanical engineer trained in tool and die, with 6–7 years in mold design. He works closely with the toolrooms that build molds, so his designs take into account how the steel will actually be cut.

He works with a small team of designers, our own injection molding machine and 3D printer, and partner toolrooms in Focal Point for CNC, VMC, EDM and wire-cut machining. You deal with one company from the first sketch to finished parts.

Design work has covered automotive parts, household appliances, FMCG products and electronic controllers.

Questions

How much does a mold design cost?

Mold designs start from $200. The price depends on the size of the part, the number of cavities and features such as slides or lifters. Send us the part and you get a fixed quote within 24 hours.

Can I order only the design?

Yes. Many clients only need the design and the checks. You get native files and STEP, and you can have the mold built by us or by any toolmaker.

Which file formats do you accept?

STEP, IGES, Parasolid, SolidWorks, STL, DWG, DXF and PDF drawings. If you only have a sample or photos, we can reverse engineer the part.

Why make a prototype first?

The mold is the most expensive step. A prototype lets you check size, fit and feel for a small cost, while changes still cost almost nothing.

How do you keep my design confidential?

We sign an NDA before receiving files, never show client work on our website or social media, and share your files only with the people working on your job.

How do we stay in touch during the project?

By WhatsApp, email or video call, whichever suits you. You speak directly with the engineer working on your part.
