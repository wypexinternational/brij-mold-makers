What we do

Design, check and build. Use one stage or all three.

Design

  • Injection mold design. Core and cavity, gates and runners, cooling, ejection, full mold assembly and 2D drawings.
  • Blow and rubber mold design. For bottles, containers, seals, grips and molded rubber parts.
  • Product design and 3D CAD. Parts, assemblies and drawings with tolerances, in SolidWorks, NX and AutoCAD.
  • Reverse engineering. From a sample, a photo or an old drawing to a clean model you can manufacture.

Check

  • Mold flow analysis. Fill, pressure, weld lines, air traps, cooling and warpage, run in Autodesk Moldflow, with a written report.
  • Design for manufacturing review. Draft, wall thickness, undercuts and parting line checked before tooling. Free with every quote.
  • 3D printed samples. FDM prints for size and fit checks. Resin prints when you need fine detail. Test the part before you pay for steel.

Build

  • Mold manufacturing. CNC, EDM and wire-cut machining in Focal Point, Jalandhar, then trials on our injection molding machine. First samples go to you.
  • CNC programming. Toolpaths and post-processors for FANUC, Siemens and Haas controls.
  • Production and shipping. Molded parts or the finished mold, packed for export and shipped to you.

Start small. Stop at any stage.

You pay stage by stage. Each stage ends with files or parts in your hands, so you can judge our work before you commit to the next one.

  1. Send us the part

    A STEP, IGES or STL file, a drawing, photos or a physical sample. If you need an NDA, we sign it before you send anything.

  2. Design and review

    We model the part or the mold and point out anything that will cause trouble in production. You get native files and STEP.

  3. Simulate and print

    We run the flow analysis and 3D print a sample. You check fit and feel before any steel is cut.

  4. Build the mold

    The mold is machined and assembled, then tried on our injection molding machine. You get first samples, with photos and video of the tool.

  5. Produce and ship

    We mold your parts or ship the mold itself, packed for export, to your address.

What a mold design covers

A cross section of a simple two-cavity injection mold. Every item below is designed, checked and drawn before machining starts.

  1. 1 Fixed half, which holds the cavity side
  2. 2 Moving half, which holds the core side
  3. 3 Sprue, where the plastic enters
  4. 4 Runner, which carries plastic to each cavity
  5. 5 Gate, sized and placed for an even fill
  6. 6 The part
  7. 7 Core
  8. 8 Cooling channels, which set the cycle time
  9. 9 Ejector pins, which push the part out
  10. 10 Parting line, where the mold opens

Who we are

Brij Mold Makers is a design and toolmaking company in Focal Point, Jalandhar, the industrial district of Punjab. We are a GST-registered manufacturer and exporter.

The design work is led by Yuvraj Sehgal. He trained in mechanical engineering with a focus on tool and die, and has spent 6–7 years designing molds and working with the toolrooms that build them. That is why our designs are made to be machined, not only to look right on screen.

Around him is a team of designers, our own injection molding machine and 3D printer, and the toolrooms of Focal Point we work with every day for CNC, EDM and wire-cut machining. You deal with one company from the first sketch to a tested mold.

Software: SolidWorks, Siemens NX and AutoCAD for design. Autodesk Moldflow for flow analysis.

Questions

Which file formats do you accept?

STEP, IGES, Parasolid, SolidWorks, STL, DWG, DXF and PDF drawings. If you only have a sample or a few photos, we can reverse engineer the part.

How much does a mold design cost?

Mold designs start from $200. The price depends on the part size, the number of cavities and features such as slides or lifters. Send us the part and you get a fixed quote within 24 hours.

Which steel do you use?

For export molds we usually use P20 tool steel. It machines well and holds up in production. If your part or volume needs a different steel, we will recommend one in the quote.

Can I order only the design?

Yes. Many clients start with the design and the flow analysis, then have the mold built by us or by a toolmaker near them. You get native files and STEP either way.

Who owns the mold?

You do, once it is paid for. We can keep it at our facility and run production for you on our molding machine, or pack it and ship it to you or your molder.

Why a 3D printed sample first?

A mold is the most expensive step. A printed sample lets you check size, fit and feel for a small cost, and changes made at this point cost almost nothing.

How do we talk during the project?

By WhatsApp, email or video call, whichever suits you. You talk directly to the engineer working on your job.

How do you keep my design confidential?

We sign an NDA before receiving files, we never show client work on our website or social media, and your files are only shared with the people working on your job.
