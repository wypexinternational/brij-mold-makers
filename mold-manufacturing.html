Mold Manufacturing

We make injection molds as per the approved design. You get the design and the mold from one company, which makes communication and changes easier.

How the Mold Is Made

  • The mold design is checked and approved before machining starts (see our design checks)
  • Machining on CNC and VMC machines
  • EDM and wire cut for fine details and sharp corners
  • Machining is done with our partner toolrooms in Focal Point, Jalandhar, under our supervision
  • Every mold is tested on our injection molding machine before dispatch

Main Parts of a Mold

  1. 1 Fixed half, which holds the cavity side
  2. 2 Moving half, which holds the core side
  3. 3 Sprue, where the plastic enters
  4. 4 Runner, which carries plastic to each cavity
  5. 5 Gate, sized and placed for an even fill
  6. 6 The part
  7. 7 Core
  8. 8 Cooling channels, which set the cycle time
  9. 9 Ejector pins, which push the part out
  10. 10 Parting line, where the mold opens

Mold Steel

We select the steel based on the production quantity. For most export molds we use P20 steel. For higher quantities or glass-filled materials, we suggest a harder steel.

Mold Ownership

The mold belongs to you after payment. We can ship it to you, or keep it with us and run production for you.

Our Process

We share photos and videos with you at each stage.

  1. Design Approval

    You approve the mold design and the flow analysis.

  2. Machining

    Machining on CNC, VMC, EDM and wire cut machines.

  3. Assembly

    The mold is assembled and the cooling and ejection are checked.

  4. Trial and Samples

    We test the mold on our machine and send you samples.

  5. Dispatch or Production

    The mold is dispatched to you, or production starts here.
