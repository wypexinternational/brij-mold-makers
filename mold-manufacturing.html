How your mold is built

  • Designed and checked first. Every mold goes through the design checks before machining starts.
  • Precision machining on CNC and VMC machines.
  • EDM and wire cut for fine details, sharp corners and deep ribs.
  • Machined with partner toolrooms in Focal Point, Jalandhar, under our supervision.
  • Tried on our own injection molding machine before anything leaves us.

What goes into a mold

  1. 1 Fixed half, which holds the cavity side
  2. 2 Moving half, which holds the core side
  3. 3 Sprue, where the plastic enters
  4. 4 Runner, which carries plastic to each cavity
  5. 5 Gate, sized and placed for an even fill
  6. 6 The part
  7. 7 Core
  8. 8 Cooling channels, which set the cycle time
  9. 9 Ejector pins, which push the part out
  10. 10 Parting line, where the mold opens

Built for a long life

We choose the steel by how many parts you need. For most export molds we use P20 tool steel, which machines well and holds up in production. For higher volumes or glass-filled plastics, we will recommend a harder steel in the quote.

The mold is yours once it is paid for. We can ship it to you or your molder, or keep it here and run production for you.

From approved design to first parts

You get photos and video at every stage, so you always know where your mold is.

  1. Design approved

    You sign off the mold design and the flow analysis.

  2. Machining

    Steel is cut on CNC, VMC, EDM and wire-cut machines.

  3. Assembly

    The mold is fitted and assembled, then the cooling and ejection are checked.

  4. Trial and samples

    We run the mold on our machine and send you the first samples.

  5. Ship or produce

    The mold ships to you, or stays here to make your parts.
