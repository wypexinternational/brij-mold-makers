Mold Design

Complete mold design: core and cavity, feed system, cooling, ejection, assembly, 2D drawings and BOM. Every design is checked for:

  • DFM

    Draft, wall thickness, undercuts and parting line.

  • Mold Flow Analysis

    Filling, pressure, weld lines and air traps, in Autodesk Moldflow.

  • Gate Location

    Even filling, no gate marks on visible faces.

  • Sink Marks

    Thick sections found and corrected.

  • Air Venting

    Vents placed to avoid burn marks and short shots.

  • Cooling and Warpage

    Cooling lines that keep parts flat and cycles short.

Mold Manufacturing

  • CNC and VMC machining
  • EDM and wire cut for fine details
  • Machined with our partner toolrooms, under our supervision
  • P20 steel for most molds, harder steels for high volumes
  • Tested on our own injection molding machine before dispatch
  • Photos and videos at every stage
  • The mold is yours after payment

Our Process

  1. Design

    Mold design and flow analysis, approved by you.

  2. Machining

    CNC, VMC, EDM and wire cut.

  3. Assembly

    Fitted, with cooling and ejection checked.

  4. Trial

    Tested on our machine. Samples sent to you.

  5. Dispatch

    Shipped to you, or kept here for production.
